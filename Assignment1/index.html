Movies I Watched This Summer

A collection of movies I watched over the course of the summer, and my thoughs on each one without giving spoilers.

Project Hail Mary

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 36m

IMDB Description

"A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship. As his memory returns, he uncovers a mission to stop a mysterious substance killing Earth's sun, and realizes that an unexpected friendship may be the key."

My Review

I really enjoyed this movie, its amazing storytelling combined with its spectacular visuals and soundtrack made for a very enjoyable experience.

The Super Mario Galaxy Movie

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 38m

IMDB Description

Mario ventures into space, exploring cosmic worlds and tackling galactic challenges far from the familiar Mushroom Kingdom.

My Review

I remember playting the origial Mario Galaxy games back on the Wii, so this gave me a lot of nostalgia. That being said, there are some parts I wasn't the biggest fan of it, I understand it had a goal of bring in both new and old fans of the games to the movie. For that reason, I can't be upset with how the movie was.

Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 12m

IMDB Description

Once a lone bounty hunter, Mandalorian Din Djarin and his apprentice Grogu embark on an exciting new Star Wars adventure.

My Review

I enjoyed the first two season of The Mandolorian, But didnt like the third season as much. While I was expecting a lot from this movie ( I am a big star wars fan ) I wasn't too disappointed with this movie. It felt like they tried to turn a entire season of the show into a movie, and thus the pacing felt a bit rushed. But the story and visuals made it a fun watch.

The Sheep Detective

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 49m

IMDB Description

Every night a shepherd reads aloud a murder mystery, pretending his sheep can understand. When he is found dead, the sheep realize at once that it was a murder and think they know everything about how to go about solving it.

My Review

This was a fun movie, its a classic murder mystery, but instead of Daniel Craig, its a bunch of sheep. No complaints

Backrooms

Rated: R
Runtime 2h 6m

IMDB Description

After a therapist's patient disappears into a dimension beyond reality, she must venture into the unknown to save him.

My Review

I didnt know a lot about the Backrooms, other then a few youtube videos and games, but I was excited to see this. The atmosphere and set design was great, I felt on edge the entire movie just waiting for something to scare me. Only problem is the charcters storys / arcs felt either rushed or non existent for me.
