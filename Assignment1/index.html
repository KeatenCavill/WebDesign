Movies I Watched This Summer

Over the summer, me and a few friends subscribed to AMC A-List. Our goal was to just simply watch as many movies as we could before the school semester starts.
Here is what I watched and my non-spoiler thoughts on them.
You can click on the title of the movie to be directed to the IMDb page for it

Project Hail Mary

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 36m

IMDB Description

"A science teacher wakes up alone on a spaceship. As his memory returns, he uncovers a mission to stop a mysterious substance killing Earth's sun, and realizes that an unexpected friendship may be the key."

My Review

I really enjoyed this movie, its amazing storytelling combined with its spectacular visuals and soundtrack made for a very enjoyable experience.

The Super Mario Galaxy Movie

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 38m

IMDB Description

Mario ventures into space, exploring cosmic worlds and tackling galactic challenges far from the familiar Mushroom Kingdom.

My Review

I remember playting the origial Mario Galaxy games back on the Wii, so this gave me a lot of nostalgia. That being said, there are some parts I wasn't the biggest fan of it, I understand it had a goal of bring in both new and old fans of the games to the movie. For that reason, I can't be upset with how the movie was.

Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 12m

IMDB Description

Once a lone bounty hunter, Mandalorian Din Djarin and his apprentice Grogu embark on an exciting new Star Wars adventure.

My Review

I enjoyed the first two season of The Mandolorian, But didnt like the third season as much. While I was expecting a lot from this movie ( I am a big star wars fan ) I wasn't too disappointed with this movie. It felt like they tried to turn a entire season of the show into a movie, and thus the pacing felt a bit rushed. But the story and visuals made it a fun watch.

The Sheep Detective

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 49m

IMDB Description

Every night a shepherd reads aloud a murder mystery, pretending his sheep can understand. When he is found dead, the sheep realize at once that it was a murder and think they know everything about how to go about solving it.

My Review

This was a fun movie, its a classic murder mystery, but instead of Daniel Craig, its a bunch of sheep. No complaints

Backrooms

Rated: R
Runtime 2h 6m

IMDB Description

After a therapist's patient disappears into a dimension beyond reality, she must venture into the unknown to save him.

My Review

I didnt know a lot about the Backrooms, other then a few youtube videos and games, but I was excited to see this. The atmosphere and set design was great, I felt on edge the entire movie just waiting for something to scare me. Only problem is the charcters storys / arcs felt either rushed or non existent for me.

The Breadwinner

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 39m

IMDB Description

When a supermom lands a Shark Tank deal, she switches roles with her breadwinner husband, who struggles to adapt as a stay-at-home dad to their three daughters.

My Review

The trailer to the movie seemed fun, but the movie itself was a bit boring instead. Some of the jokes fell flat, and I wasnt too invested into the story either. That being said, I wouldn't say it was the worst movie I have seen this summer, It fills its role as a fun family movie, but that is about it.

Scary Movie (2026)

Rated: R
Runtime 1h 35m

IMDB Description

Two friends find themselves caught up in mayhem involving killers, monsters and supernatural creatures once again.

My Review

Let me state this first, I have not seen any of the other Scary Movies. I knew it was a adult parody of the scream movies (which I also haven't seen). It was something my friends were more invested in and brought me along to watch. The style of humor was not for me, but my friends had a fun time so I can't knock the movie for that.

Masters Of The Universe

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 20m

IMDB Description

Prince Adam discovers that the strange memories shaping his life are real when the lost Sword of Power resurfaces, forcing him to return to Eternia and challenge the tyrant who shattered his family and kingdom..

My Review

This movie reminded me a lot of Thor: ragnorak, a really fun sci-fi / fantasy action movie with some really fun moments. As much as I don't like Jared Letto, his skeletor was the best part of the movie in my opinion. Dispite never seening any of the origial cartoon show, this movie managed to get me so invested into the world that I binged watch a lot of youtube videos about the original shows. Highly recommend.

Lucky Strikes

Rated: R
Runtime 1h 43m

IMDB Description

A wounded American soldier is trapped behind German lines during the Battle of the Bulge.

My Review

This was part of the AMC Screen Unseen showings. They do these every monday, only telling you the rating and runtime of the movie, so you are going in blind. I'm not a big history guy, but this movie did managed to get me intrigued and invested into the character. Had some great action and fun twists.

Disclosure Day

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 25m

IMDB Description

If you found out we weren't alone, if someone showed you, proved it to you, would that frighten you?

My Review

I was disappointed with this one. It had some fun action scenes, but the story and paceing made very little sense to me. There isn't much I can say about this one, I walked out of the showing with very mixed feelings about it.

The Death of Robin Hood

Rated: R
Runtime 2h 2m

IMDB Description

Grappling with his past after a life of crime and murder, Robin Hood finds himself gravely injured after a battle he thought would be his last. In the hands of a mysterious woman, he is offered a chance at salvation.

My Review

This could be a me issue, but going from the trailers for this movie I was expecting something very different. Its a fine movie, Hugh Jackman is a favorite actor of mine, and his portrayal of an older Robin Hood was great, I just was expecting something different.

Supergirl

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 1h 48m

IMDB Description

Kara Zor-El, aka Supergirl, joins forces with an unlikely companion on an interstellar journey of vengeance and justice when an unexpected adversary strikes too close to home.

My Review

I had a fun time with this movie. The story was fun, the action was great, and the actors did a great job. It had some amazing visuals and great music.

Jackass: Best and Last

Rated: R
Runtime 1h 32m

IMDB Description

Follows the Jackass crew as they perform their final series of dangerous stunts and pranks, marking the end of the franchise.

My Review

Similar to Scary Movie (2026), I have not seen any of the other Jackass movies / show, and it was something that my friends were more excited about then me. While some of the stunts got me interested, its another case of simply "My Humor isnt a match for this movie". That being said, my friends had a great time.

Minions & Monsters

Rated: PG
Runtime 1h 30m

IMDB Description

Follows the Minions in 1920s Hollywood as they search for frightening creatures for their monster movie, partner with a green creature, and must save the planet after unleashing monsters.

My Review

This was a fun movie, its simple and light hearted and obviously aimed for a younger audience, yet it had some jokes even for a older audience. The story works for what it is and I really can't complain about it.

The Odyssey

Rated: R
Runtime 2h 52m

IMDB Description

After the Trojan War, Odysseus faces a dangerous voyage back to Ithaca, meeting creatures like the Cyclops Polyphemus, Sirens, and Calypso along the way.

My Review

To start, I am a big fan of the original geek Epic, And this movie was a fun recreation. Its not a one to one recreation, they skip over stuff and add other stuff to make the story work in modern cinema. There are some aspect that they added that I thing describe aspect better then the original epic. I did really enjoy this movie though.

Hadestown: The Musical (ProShot)

Rated: NA
Runtime 2h 21m

IMDB Description

A musical juxtaposition of the Orpheus/Eurydice and Hades/Persephone myths that examines the way real life can impact our quest for a perfect world.

My Review

I knew of the musical, yet never listen to it before hand, so I went into this completely blind. I was completely blown away by it, as soon as the showing ended, I added roughly 6 of the songs from the soundtack to my spotify playlist. The music an story were great and the cinematography was spectacular. Probobly my favorite movie I saw all summer.

One Night Only

Rated: R
Runtime 1h 42m

IMDB Description

Two New Yorkers search for love on the one night of the year when pre-marital sex is legal.

My Review

Another AMC Screen Unseen when I watched it. While I'm not the biggest fan of RomComs and the basic plot of the movie didn't hook me entirely, It wasn't a bad movie. There were some fun moments and some great lines, and some not so great moments that make no sense to me. However if you are a fan of RomComs like my friend that I went and watched it with, you will enjoy it. He ended up watching it another 2 times after the movie offically came out.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 2h 25m

IMDB Description

A forgotten Peter Parker lives alone as a full-time Spider-Man until mounting pressure triggers a dangerous change and a powerful new enemy emerges.

My Review

I really like the MCU Spider-man movies, and I was really currious on how they were gunna handle it after "No Way Home". I really enjoy what they did, while its not my favorite Tom Holland Spider-Man movie, I still had a fun time watching it.

The End of Oak Street

Rated: PG-13
Runtime 1h 39m

IMDB Description

The Platt family bands together to navigate their new surroundings after a cosmic event transports their suburban neighborhood to someplace unknown.

My Review

The Last Movie of the summer, I thought it was alright. It had some fun moments and other intenese moments, I can best describe this movie as a modern day "Jurrasic Park". My one main problem for it, is that they never describe why the event of the movie happen in the first place. That was something that stuck with me the entire movie and to have it not answered by the end just made me annoyed.
